Leaving Cert French Opinion Piece

A clear structure, the phrases that lift an answer and a Higher Level sample answer, so you can write a strong opinion piece on any topic.

Written exam guide Opinion piece Journal intime Letters & emails

The opinion piece rewards thinking, not memory. Examiners want a clear position, two or three developed points and French that is accurate. A learned-off essay forced onto the wrong question won’t become relevant just because the French is impressive.

A structure that works for any topic

  1. Open with your position. One or two sentences that respond to the exact statement and say where you stand. No long memorised introduction.
  2. Make two or three developed points. For each one, give the point, explain why, and add an example. One developed point is worth more than three bare ones.
  3. Acknowledge the other side. A sentence with Certes…, mais… shows maturity and gives you a natural place for a contrast.
  4. Close briefly. Sum up your view in a sentence. Don’t introduce a new idea at the end.

Phrases that lift an opinion piece

Learn these as building blocks you can drop into any topic, not as a script.

  • À mon avis… / Selon moi…In my opinion…
  • Il est vrai que…It is true that…
  • Tout d’abord… Ensuite… Enfin…First of all… Next… Finally…
  • De plus… / En outre…Moreover… / Furthermore…
  • Certes…, mais…Admittedly…, but…
  • Cependant… / Pourtant…However… / And yet…
  • Bien que + subjonctifAlthough…
  • Je suis convaincu(e) que…I am convinced that…
  • Bref… / Pour conclure…In short… / To conclude…

Higher Level sample answer

Opinion piece

« Les pochettes pour les portables à l’école : une bonne idée ? »

Phone pouches in school: a good idea?

Sample answer

À mon avis, les pochettes pour les portables, qu’on a introduites dans de nombreuses écoles irlandaises, sont une excellente initiative. Tout d’abord, elles permettent aux élèves de se concentrer en classe : sans notifications toutes les deux minutes, on apprend beaucoup mieux. De plus, la pause de midi est redevenue un moment de vraie conversation. Au lieu de faire défiler TikTok, on se parle ! Certes, certains parents s’inquiètent de ne pas pouvoir joindre leurs enfants en cas d’urgence. Cependant, le secrétariat reste toujours joignable. Bref, bien que ce soit un peu frustrant au début, je suis convaincue que ces pochettes rendent l’école plus saine et plus sociable.

Notice the shape: position, two developed points, the other side, a short conclusion. Use the structure on any topic, but write your own ideas.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should a Leaving Cert French opinion piece be?

Follow the word guidance on your paper. In the established Higher Level format, the compulsory 60-mark question asks for roughly 90 words. Going far over doesn’t earn extra marks and gives you more chances to make mistakes.

Should I learn opinion pieces off by heart?

No. Learn flexible phrases and topic vocabulary instead. A memorised essay often doesn’t fit the exact statement on the paper, and an answer that misses the question loses marks however good the French is.
