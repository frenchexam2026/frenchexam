Why a workbook, not a book of essays?

Most rédaction resources hand you a finished essay to learn off. The Essay Builder does something different — it takes you through the process of building one yourself.

📚

Topic Vocabulary

Each rédaction opens with the key vocabulary you actually need for that topic — not a generic word list, but language built for the essay ahead.

🔁

Translation Practice

Short translation activities bridge the gap between knowing a word and being able to use it accurately in a sentence.

🧱

Sentence Building

Guided sentence-building exercises help you construct stronger, more complex French — the kind that earns marks at Higher Level.

💬

Useful Expressions

Topic-specific connectors and expressions are introduced in context, so they're ready to use, not just recognised.

✍️

Write Your Own

With the groundwork done, you write your own version of the rédaction — the actual skill the exam is testing.

✅

Model Answers & Corrections

Every topic finishes with a full model answer and corrections, so you can compare your work and see exactly what a strong response looks like.

Frequently asked questions

Is this just a book of essays to learn off?

No. The Essay Builder is a workbook, not a set of essays to memorise. Each of the 25 topics includes vocabulary, translation and sentence-building activities that build the language you need before you see the model answer.

Does it include model answers?

Yes. Every topic includes a full model answer and corrections, so you can check your own work and see what a strong, polished response looks like.

The PDF is a digital download available immediately after purchase. The printed edition is a physical copy posted within Ireland, with delivery taking approximately two to three weeks.
