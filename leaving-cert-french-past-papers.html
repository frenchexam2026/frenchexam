Leaving Cert French Past Papers & Marking Schemes

Where to find the official papers, and how to use them properly — from an SEC oral examiner and corrector.

The official Leaving Cert French past papers and marking schemes are free on examinations.ie, in the State Examinations Commission’s Exam Material Archive. The papers are only half the story, though: students who improve most use the marking scheme to correct every answer and work out exactly why marks were lost.

Where to find official Leaving Cert French past papers

  1. Go to examinations.ie, the official website of the State Examinations Commission (SEC).
  2. Open the Exam Material Archive and accept the terms.
  3. Choose Exam Papers (or Marking Schemes), then the year, Leaving Certificate and French.
  4. Download the Higher Level (or Ordinary Level) paper — and the marking scheme for the same year so you can correct your work.

You’ll find copies of the papers on other websites too, but the SEC archive is the official, free source — and it’s where you’ll find the marking schemes.

Which past papers should you start with?

Start with the most recent papers. They are closest in style to the exam you’ll sit, so they give you the most realistic practice. Save one or two recent papers for full timed mocks closer to June, and use older papers for section-by-section practice during the year.

How to use a past paper properly — section by section

📖 Reading comprehension

Do one text at a time against the clock. Underline the evidence in the text for every answer before you write it, then correct it with the marking scheme — the scheme shows how precise your answer needs to be.

How to use the marking scheme

The marking scheme is the most under-used study tool in Leaving Cert French. Use it to see which answers are accepted, how much detail earns full marks, and how grammar and manipulation questions are rewarded. For written work, compare your answer with what examiners reward: relevance to the task, clear communication, accurate language and the right register.

Be strict with yourself. If your answer wouldn’t clearly earn the mark, it doesn’t get it.

Common past paper mistakes

  • Learning answers off. The same questions won’t come up again — practise the method, not the answers.
  • Only doing your favourite sections. The marks are spread across all sections, so practise the ones you avoid.
  • Never timing yourself. Timing is a skill — practise it before June, not during the exam.
  • Marking too generously. Use the marking scheme honestly, or you won’t know where marks are really going.

A simple past paper routine

From September to Christmas, do one past paper section a week. After Christmas, add a full section under timed conditions every week. In the final weeks, sit one or two complete papers as full mocks. Our Leaving Cert French study plan shows how to fit this into a realistic week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find Leaving Cert French past papers?

The official papers are published free by the State Examinations Commission (SEC) on examinations.ie, in the Exam Material Archive. Choose exam papers, the year, Leaving Certificate and French to download the paper you want.

Are Leaving Cert French marking schemes available?

Yes. The SEC publishes marking schemes alongside the exam papers in the same archive — choose marking schemes instead of exam papers when you search. They show the answers and the criteria examiners use.

How many past papers should I do for Leaving Cert French?

Quality matters more than quantity. Working through the last five or six years properly — timed, corrected with the marking scheme and followed up — is worth far more than skimming every paper ever set.

Should I learn past paper answers off by heart?

No. The same texts and questions won’t come up again. Use past papers to practise the method — finding evidence in the text, manipulating grammar, planning written answers — so you can apply it to any paper.
