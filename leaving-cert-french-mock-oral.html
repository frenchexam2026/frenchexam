Mock oral FAQ

What happens in a mock oral session?

We hold a 30-minute online conversation in French, run like the real Leaving Cert oral, covering the kinds of topics you can expect on the day. Afterwards you receive written notes on what went well and exactly what to improve before your exam.

How do I book?

Email bonjour@frenchexam.ie with the student’s name, level and a few times that suit in the week before the oral. You’ll get a reply with a time and a payment link.

How much does it cost?

€30 for a 30-minute online session, including written feedback notes afterwards.

What do I need?

A quiet room, a laptop, tablet or phone with a camera and microphone, and a stable internet connection.
