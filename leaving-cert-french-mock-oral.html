Mock oral FAQ

What happens in a mock oral session?

We hold a 30-minute online conversation in French, run like the real Leaving Cert oral, covering the kinds of topics you can expect on the day. Afterwards you receive written notes on what went well and exactly what to improve before your exam.

How do I book?

Email bonjour@frenchexam.ie with the student’s name, level and which day and times suit (Friday 19 March afternoon, Saturday 20 or Sunday 21 March 2027). You’ll get a reply with a time and a payment link.

How much does it cost?

€30 for a 30-minute online session, including written feedback notes afterwards. Students on the FrenchExam.ie course pay €25 — just mention it when you book.

When are the mock orals?

Friday 19 March 2027 (afternoon only, very limited places), and all day Saturday 20 and Sunday 21 March 2027.

What do I need?

A quiet room, a laptop, tablet or phone with a camera and microphone, and a stable internet connection.
